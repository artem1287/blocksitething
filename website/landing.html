Why hard blocks don't stick

A wall invites you to knock it down. A ramp doesn't.

Freedom, Cold Turkey, and most blockers give you one lever: on or off. The willpower cost of "off" is fixed and low — one click, one password, one "just this once" — so it gets pulled the first bad day. A shrinking allowance has no single moment that feels like giving up. There's always still some time left; there's just less of it than yesterday.

Hard block

100% access, then 0%, the moment a schedule or a password stands between you and the site. One disable and the whole thing is off.

Taper's allowance

A little less every day, front-loaded so day one already feels different, holding at a maintenance floor you choose instead of chasing zero.

How it works

Three decisions, then it runs itself.

Name your worst offender

The one site you actually can't put down. It gets a hard block during hours you set — no allowance, no exceptions — starting day one, whichever pace you pick.

Pick your pace

Gentle (15% off, then 15%/week), Moderate (20%, then 25%/week), or Aggressive (25%, then 40%/week). Every tier front-loads a real cut on day one — you don't wait a week to see anything change.

Watch your allowance shrink

When today's minutes run out, you land on a screen that tells you what to do instead — not just a wall. One "I need a few more minutes" pass per week, with a short wait built in, for the days that are genuinely hard.

Where this stands

No customers yet — that's the point of this build.

I'm building Taper because every blocker I tried was a light switch, and my actual problem was never "can I turn this off" — it was that turning it off was too easy. Nothing here is proven at scale yet; it's a working extension I use myself and want a handful of other people to genuinely try before it's anything more than that.

FAQ

Before you install it

Will it actually block me?

Yes, but expect it to feel different from Freedom or Cold Turkey. There's no wall on day one for most sites — just a smaller allowance than you're used to, shrinking further each day. Your one named "worst offender" does get a real, unconditional hard block during hours you set.

What if I have a real emergency?

The escape valve: one pass per rolling seven days, account-wide. It asks what's going on, makes you wait about ten minutes, then grants a bonus for that day only — tomorrow's allowance is completely unaffected.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet. This test build is a Chrome/Edge/Firefox extension only — desktop browsing. A mobile version is a later, separate decision, not something in scope right now.

Is my browsing data private?

Your usage data stays on your device — there's no account or server sync in this build, and nothing about which sites you visit is transmitted anywhere. See the privacy note in the footer for the full picture.

Privacy How it works FAQ

Privacy, plainly

This is an early test build with no company, account system, or server behind it yet. Here's exactly what that means today:

What stays on your device

Which sites you've blocked, your schedule or taper settings, and how much time you've spent on tracked sites are stored locally in your browser (chrome.storage) and never transmitted anywhere — not to a server, not to an analytics tool, not to an ad network. Uninstalling the extension deletes it.

What I do collect

Nothing automatically. If you email feedback or a bug report, I have whatever you sent me and your email address, kept only to reply to you.

What changes later

A future paid version will need an account and a server to handle sync and billing — at that point this policy gets rewritten to say exactly what's stored there, before it applies to you.
